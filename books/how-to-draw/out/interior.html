How to Use This Book

1
Pick a drawing. Start with anything you like — there are 100 to choose from!
2
Follow the steps. In each box, the dark black lines are what you draw next. The light grey lines are what you already drew.
3
Draw lightly with a pencil. Small, soft lines are easy to erase and fix.
4
Use the big box. Every page has a space that says Your Turn! — draw it there.
5
Add color! Go over your lines with a marker, then color it in any way you want.
6
Mistakes are OK! Every artist practices. Try again and it gets easier every time.
light grey = already drawn
→
black = draw this now
You will need: a pencil • an eraser • crayons, colored pencils or markers
