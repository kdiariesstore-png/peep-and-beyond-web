How to Draw Like a Pro

1
Start with guidelines. Every drawing begins with dashed guide lines: simple circles, ovals and cross lines that show where everything goes. Draw them very lightly.
2
Follow the steps. The black lines show what to draw in each step. The grey lines are what you have already drawn. Read the short instruction under each box.
3
Erase the guidelines. In the last step the guidelines disappear. Erase yours too, and your drawing will look clean and professional.
4
Practise on the next page. Every drawing has its own full practice page with a light grid to help you get the size and shape right.
5
Check the difficulty. Look for the stars: ★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenge.
