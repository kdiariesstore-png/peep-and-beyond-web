Ready to level up your drawing?

This book teaches the same trick real artists use: start with simple guidelines, build up the shapes step by step, then erase the guides for a clean, professional finish. Every step has a short, clear instruction.

  • 6-7 steps per drawing with easy-to-follow instructions
  • A full practice page with a proportion grid after every drawing
  • Difficulty stars, Pro Tips, warm-up exercises and bonus challenges
  • Big 8.5 x 11 in pages and a Pro Artist certificate at the end
